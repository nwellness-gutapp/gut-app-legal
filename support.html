Support

Need help with florae, or have feedback, a bug report, or a question about your account? Email us at hello@floraeapp.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • How do I delete a habit, symptom, or food entry? Swipe left on it in its list to reveal Edit and Delete.
  • How is my vitality/gut score calculated? It's a 0–100 blend of your habit completion and fibre-goal progress over the last 7 days — see the (i) next to your score on the Dashboard for details.
  • How do I delete my account or export my data? Settings → Account. Data export is available before deletion.
  • Something not working, or a question not answered here? Email hello@floraeapp.com — a real person reads every message.
